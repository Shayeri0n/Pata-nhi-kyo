A Story That Stayed

Pata Nahi Kyon...

Kuch mohabbatein mukammal nahi hoti.

Maine mohabbat mein uski tasveer banayi thi,
Usne use bas ek dost ki nishani samajh kar rakh liya… Pata nahi kyon.
Main uske saath khel mein har baazi jeet jaata tha,
Magar ishq ki baazi mein khud hi haar gaya… Pata nahi kyon.
Maine toh mohabbat mein uski kundali tak mila li thi,
Aur usne shayad meri kismat mein
mera naam hi nahi likha tha… Pata nahi kyon.
Main har baar uski khamoshi mein
apne liye koi ishara dhoondhta raha,
Woh har baar meri mohabbat ko
sirf dosti samajhti rahi… Pata nahi kyon.
Na uski koi khata thi,
Na meri mohabbat mein koi kami thi,
Bas hum dono ki kismat ki
kahani thodi alag thi…

Pata nahi kyon…

Some stories don't need an ending.
VARUN
